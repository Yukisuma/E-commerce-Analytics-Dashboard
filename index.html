💡 Data-Driven Recommendations
Strategic insights from 12,847 transactions · 8,742 customers · 5 channels · Jan–Sep 2024
01
🏆 Scale the Loyalty Program
Loyalty Program delivers the highest ROAS (18.2×) and conversion rate (6.2%) of all campaigns. Champions segment (1,245 customers) averages $387 AOV — 75% above store average. Recommend a 40% budget increase and tiered VIP tiers to graduate Loyal → Champion customers.
✦ Projected Revenue Impact: +$234K
02
📧 Double Down on Email Marketing
Email delivers a 17.5× ROAS with the lowest CAC at $19.20 — 3.8× cheaper than Google Ads ($73.80). Email conversion rate of 5.9% outperforms all paid channels. Build RFM-based drip sequences targeting each customer segment with personalized offers.
✦ Projected Revenue Impact: +$189K at same spend
03
🛒 Fix the Cart Abandonment Crisis
73.9% of shoppers abandon after adding to cart — the largest funnel drop-off. Implement a 3-email abandonment flow (1hr, 24hr, 72hr) with a 10% incentive trigger. Add trust signals, one-click checkout, and mobile-optimized UX (67% of traffic is mobile).
✦ Projected Revenue Impact: +$284K recovered
04
⚠️ Re-engage At-Risk Customers
987 customers are classified At-Risk with declining purchase frequency (current AOV: $134, down from $198). Launch a personalized win-back campaign with product recommendations based on past behavior. A 30% win-back rate at $165 AOV generates significant incremental value.
✦ Projected Revenue Impact: +$48K
05
🔗 Launch a Formal Referral Program
Referral channel converts at 6.7% (highest of all channels) with a 14.6× ROAS — yet receives only $12K spend. Referral customers tend to be higher quality. Launch a structured referral program with rewards for both referrer and new customer to scale this organically.
✦ Projected Revenue Impact: +$156K
06
📊 Rebalance Google Ads Budget
Google Ads generates $847K revenue but at 4.03× ROAS and $73.80 CAC — the weakest paid efficiency. Shift 15% of Google Ads budget to Email and Organic SEO. Keep Google Ads focused on branded keywords and retargeting, which typically achieve 3× higher ROAS.
✦ Projected Revenue Impact: +$73K efficiency gain
